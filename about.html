Our story

Started in Chicago. Built on quality.

Coy began three years ago with a single ladder, a bucket, and a belief that exterior cleaning should be done with the same care you'd give your own home. Founded in Chicago by a small crew of detail-obsessed window cleaners, we earned our first customers the hard way — knocking on doors in our own neighborhoods.

Today we serve homes across the Chicagoland area, but the approach hasn't changed. Small team. Careful hands. Honest pricing. Every customer treated like a neighbor, because most of them are.
