Frequently asked

The questions we hear most.

Straight answers about how we work, what to expect, and how to book.

01What sets Coy apart from other window cleaners?
We don't rush. Every job follows the same careful routine — pre-rinse, hand-scrub the glass, squeegee with a clean edge, and detail the frames and sills before we leave. The result is glass that actually looks clean up close, not just from the driveway.
02Can you reach second and third-story windows?
Yes. Our crew is trained on extension ladders and water-fed pole systems for tall or hard-to-reach glass. Safety equipment and proper setup come standard, so even the tricky panes get the same care as the ones at eye level.
03How often should I have my windows cleaned?
For most homes, twice a year — once in spring and once in fall — keeps the glass consistently clear. Homes near construction, busy roads, or heavy tree cover often benefit from quarterly visits. We'll recommend a cadence based on what you're dealing with.
04What happens if it rains right after you clean?
Clean glass shrugs off normal rain just fine — water beads and runs without leaving the streaks dirty glass would. If a storm somehow leaves visible spotting within a few days of your service, give us a call and we'll come back to make it right.
05Do you offer discounts for repeat customers?
Yes. Customers on a recurring schedule get a reduced rate on every visit, and we hold your appointment slot so you don't have to chase the booking each season. Ask us for current pricing when you reach out.
06How do I book a cleaning?
The easiest way is to grab a free quote with the button above — tell us a bit about your home and we'll get back to you with a price the same day. You can also call us directly using the Call Us tab at the top of the page.
