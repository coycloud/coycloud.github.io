Questions

Holiday Lighting: FAQ.

How much does holiday light installation cost?
Every home is different, so we quote each one individually. Price depends on how much of the roofline you want lit, the number of stories, and whether you add trees, bushes, wreaths, or garland. Quotes are free and we usually reply the same day.
When should I book?
As early as you can. Install dates fill up quickly from November into early December, so booking early gets you the date you want and lights up before your first holiday gathering.
Do I need to buy my own lights?
No. We supply commercial-grade LED lights and clips as part of the service, so there's nothing for you to buy, test, or untangle.
Will the installation damage my roof or gutters?
No. We hang lights with clips made for gutters and shingles, never nails or staples, so your roofline is left exactly as we found it.
What if a bulb or strand goes out?
Tell us and we'll come back and fix it at no charge for the rest of the season.
Do you take the lights down?
Yes. Takedown after the holidays is included. We remove everything, pack it up, and store it for next year.
