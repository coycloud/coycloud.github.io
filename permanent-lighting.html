Questions

Permanent lighting: FAQ.

Can you see the lights during the day?
Very little. The track sits tucked under the soffit or along the fascia and is matched to your trim color, so it blends into the house. Most neighbors only notice the lights once they're on.
Do I need to take them down after the holidays?
No. They're designed to stay up year-round. Use them for every holiday, then switch to warm white or soft accent lighting the rest of the year.
How do I control them?
From an app on your phone. You can pick colors and patterns, save favorite scenes, and set schedules like "on at sunset, off at 11pm." We set it all up with you at the end of the install.
How much does it cost?
Pricing is based mainly on the linear feet of roofline you want lit, plus the number of stories and how easy the roof is to reach. The consult and quote are free, and we'll give you one clear price for the full install.
Does it work on two- and three-story homes?
Yes. Our crew already works on multi-story homes every week for gutter and window cleaning, and we bring the right ladders and safety setup for taller rooflines.
Where does the power come from?
Usually from an existing exterior outlet, often in the garage or near the eaves. We'll find the cleanest route during the consult so wiring stays out of sight.
