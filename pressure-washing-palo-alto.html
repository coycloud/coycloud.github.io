How often should Palo Alto homes get pressure washing?
Once a year is right for most homes. Shaded, damp areas that grow mildew or moss may need it twice — once after the rainy season and once before it.
Will pressure washing damage my pavers or stone?
Not when it's done right. We match pressure to the surface, and for delicate exteriors like siding and roofs we use low-pressure soft washing instead.
